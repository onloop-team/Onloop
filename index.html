Questions

Good to know before you order.

How does Restoq work?

You choose the items or bundles you need, review your basket, and send the order to Restoq on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, delivery, and payment with you.

Do I pay on the website?

No. For now, payment is handled manually on WhatsApp after we confirm your order.

Can homes and businesses order?

Yes. Restoq supports household restocks, office essentials, and staff welfare packages.

Can I change a bundle?

Yes. Bundles are only a starting point. You can add more items or remove anything before checkout.

What areas do you deliver to?

Delivery areas will be confirmed on WhatsApp during the pilot phase.
